A little guidance for the journey

You belong here.

What is a discipleship band?

A same-gender circle of 3–5 adults meeting weekly for Scripture, prayer, honest sharing, and encouragement. Begin with six months. Allow approximately 15 minutes per participant, plus time for prayer. Practice equal sharing, no unsolicited advice, and Challenge by Choice.

Do I need to belong to a church?

No. Independent participants can create and join bands. Church affiliation is optional.

Can I participate internationally?

Yes. Online Worldwide works across countries without location disclosure. Meeting times convert to the IANA time zone in your profile and account for daylight saving changes.

How do I join or start a band?

Register, complete your profile, and send a short introduction to a suitable band. The host approves membership. Three approved members unlock the private workspace; recruitment can stay open until capacity is reached. You can also start a new band independently.

What if location permission is denied?

Enter your country and city manually or choose Online Worldwide. For nearby search you may enter approximate coordinates manually and enable location discovery. You can disable it at any time.

How is my privacy protected?

Use a limited display name and leave your church blank if identification would create risk. Profile locations, full names, and email addresses are never in the directory. Bands list only approximate public areas. Exact meeting details require approved membership in a band of at least three.

Confidentiality has safeguarding limits when someone faces harm. This application is not an emergency service. Seek trusted local assistance in an emergency.

I can't find a band. Is connection support available?

This release has no staffed connection service or support queue. Broaden your search or start a band. Connection assistance and church administration are planned for Phase 3.

What is included in this release?

Phase 1 includes persistent accounts, profiles, discovery, band creation, join requests, approvals, capacity protection, private meeting details, in-app notifications, and account deletion. Real password-recovery email needs an operator-configured SMTP service. Later-phase community, moderation, mapping, and video features are not yet implemented.
